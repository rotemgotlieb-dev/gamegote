Gote Desert

A desert that remembers what you did to it. Walk it, carve into a dune, and the sand collapses, slides, and settles back to the angle at which dry sand stops moving. Every grain of it is generated at load. Nothing here is a texture someone painted, and the music is written by the page as it plays.
