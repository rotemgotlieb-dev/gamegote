REPOSE

A desert that remembers what you did to it. Every dune, every ripple and every grain of shading is generated on the GPU at load; nothing here is a texture or a model someone drew.

Carve into a slope and the sand does not just dent. It collapses, slides, and settles back to its angle of repose, the roughly 34 degrees at which dry sand stops sliding. That behaviour is the whole point of the demo.

Before you click

Desktop Chrome or Edge. This runs on WebGPU, which phones and Safari do not reliably support yet, and the controls need a trackpad or mouse. On an unsupported browser you will get a one-line message rather than a picture, which means it is working as designed, not broken.

Three versions, so you can see it being built

Each is one self-contained file. Nothing is downloaded after the page loads.

Milestone 0 Foundation The engine, the camera, and the measuring instruments. Almost nothing to look at on purpose. Milestone 1 Terrain and light The erg, the golden-hour sky, and the six-layer sand material. Beautiful and completely static. Milestone 2 Deformation and carve Start here The sand becomes real. Carve it, pour it, watch avalanches run down a slipface and stop.

Controls

All of it is trackpad-native. There is no menu you have to find first.

  • press + dragCarve
  • two-finger scrollOrbit the basin
  • pinchMove in and out, 8 to 80 m
  • F1 or `Open the settings panel

The settings panel is where the demo shows its working. It has the four camera positions the whole project is graded against, a switch for each layer of the sand material so you can turn one off and see exactly what it was contributing, the wet-sand toggle, the brush size and the angle of repose itself, and the freezes that stop time so a frame can be measured.

What is real yet, and what is not

Working now

  • Carving, and sand that avalanches back to its own repose angle instead of holding an impossible wall.
  • Pouring, which builds a cone through discrete slides rather than growing smoothly.
  • Carves that cast their own shadows, because the deformation is real geometry and not a decal.
  • Wet sand: darker, stands steeper, crumbles as it dries.

Not built yet

  • The bending. Lifting a mass of sand into the air and throwing it is the centrepiece of the project and it is two milestones away. Right now you can only shape the ground.
  • Wind, drifting ripples, and the blowing saltation layer.
  • The post-processing chain, so the image is raw. No bloom, no depth of field, no film grain.
REPOSE is a technical demo, built in the browser with WebGPU. No textures, meshes or images ship with it.
